Who We Are

A Specialist Fastener Manufacturer with Global Reach

Datong Ningxin Huida Technology Development Co., Ltd. is a precision fastener and custom metal parts manufacturer. We produce standard and non-standard fasteners, special-shaped parts, riveting fasteners, self-tapping screws and lean casting components — serving aerospace, railway, construction and industrial OEM customers across Europe, the Americas and Asia.

With production roots dating back to 2020 in Handan and a modern facility established in Datong in 2025, we combine mature manufacturing capability with continuous investment in quality, testing and technical innovation.

  • Dual production bases in Handan and Datong, China
  • ISO 9001 · 14001 · 45001 certified management systems
  • In-house testing, inspection and utility-model patent
  • Trusted by CRRC, COMAC, AVIC and other industry leaders

Our Journey

Milestones & Growth

A steady path from a single plant to a certified, dual-base fastener manufacturer.

NOV 2020

Handan Plant Founded

Ningxin Huida begins fastener manufacturing in Handan, Hebei, laying the foundation for standard fastener production and process expertise.

2021 — 2024
