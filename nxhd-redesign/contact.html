Before You Ask

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers on ordering, samples, delivery and customization.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

Our MOQ is flexible — typically 1,000 pcs for standard fasteners and as low as 100 pcs for non-standard or special-shaped parts. For prototypes and new projects we can often start even lower. Tell us your requirement and we will quote accordingly.

What is your typical delivery time?

Standard fasteners usually ship within 7–15 days. Custom and non-standard parts typically take 15–30 days depending on complexity, tooling and quantity. We will confirm a clear lead time with your quotation.

Can I get samples before placing a bulk order?

Yes. We provide samples for evaluation — free for existing stock items (you cover courier cost); custom samples are charged for tooling, which can be refunded against your first bulk order.

Do you manufacture custom / non-standard fasteners?

Absolutely. Custom and special-shaped parts are our core strength. Send us your drawings (PDF/CAD/STEP) or a physical sample, and our engineers will advise on material, tolerance and cost, then produce to your exact specification.

What standards do you follow?

We manufacture to DIN, ISO, ANSI/ASME, GB and JIS standards, and can also work to your company-specific drawings and specifications. Please state the standard and grade in your inquiry.

What certifications and quality controls do you have?

We are ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 certified and hold Russian GOST R certification, an AAA credit rating and a utility-model patent. Every batch is inspected in-house — hardness, dimensional, tensile and salt-spray testing — with reports available on request.

What payment terms do you accept?

We accept T/T (bank transfer), typically 30% deposit with 70% balance before shipment. For long-term partners we offer flexible terms. Other methods can be discussed case by case.

Do you offer OEM / ODM and private labeling?

Yes. We support OEM and ODM, including custom packaging, labeling and branding. Our engineers work with you from design and prototyping through to mass production.
