Our three ISO management systems — **ISO 9001:2015 (Quality)**, **ISO 14001:2015 (Environment)** and **ISO 45001:2018 (Occupational H&S)** — were re-certified this quarter following the surveillance audit of both manufacturing plants (Handan, founded 2020, and Datong HQ, founded 2025).

## What the audit covered

- Incoming material inspection for carbon steel, stainless steel and alloy wire/rod
- Heat-treatment, surface-finishing and packaging process control
- Calibration of tensile / torque / hardness testing equipment
- Documented traceability from raw-material batch to finished goods
- Hazard identification for cold-heading, thread-rolling and salt-spray operations

The audit closed with **zero major non-conformities**. Updated certificates have been published to the Quality page — please request the latest PDFs from your account manager if you need them on file for supplier approval.

## What it means for customers

- **Documented material traceability** on every shipment, including batch heat numbers and mechanical test results.
- **Process consistency** validated by external surveillance — useful when your supplier-quality team asks for an audit report.
- **Environment & H&S controls** documented per ISO 14001 / 45001 — required by some EU and APAC OEMs.
