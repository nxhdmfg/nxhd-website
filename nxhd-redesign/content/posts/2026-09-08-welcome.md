We are excited to launch the **Ningxin Huida newsroom** — a place where we share engineering know-how, manufacturing updates, and the stories behind the fasteners that hold critical structures together.

## What you'll find here

- **Engineering deep-dives** — practical guidance on selecting standard, special and self-tapping fasteners for demanding applications.
- **Quality & testing** — notes from our ISO 9001 / 14001 / 45001 audited processes, including tensile, torque and salt-spray testing.
- **Industry case studies** — how our parts are integrated into railway, aerospace, construction and industrial OEM projects.

## How to get in touch

If you'd like a quote, a technical review of an existing drawing, or a CoC/test report, drop us a message at **[contact@nxhdmfg.com](mailto:contact@nxhdmfg.com)** — typical response time is **under 24 hours**.

We post new articles roughly **once a week**, with longer technical pieces roughly every two weeks. Thanks for reading — and welcome aboard.
